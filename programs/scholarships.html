Tips for Global Opportunities Applications

  • Start your search and application process early.
  • Prepare all required documents (transcripts, recommendations, essays).
  • Tailor your application and essays to each opportunity’s goals.
  • Highlight your achievements, leadership, and community involvement.
  • Double-check deadlines and submit before the due date.
